# Form spec: Medical Center Check-In, Staff / Crew Members

Source paper form: "MEDICAL CENTER CHECK IN STAFF/CREW MEMBERS".
Filled by: medical leader, for each crew member / volunteer / staff, before camp.
Maps to: crew_medical_checkins (Tier 2). Parallel to the camper arrival check but NOT a
reskin. Crew have fields campers do not. Build it as its own form.
Form wins over the brief. No em dashes.

## Identity
- Name
- DOB

## Assessment (note the two crew-specific fields)
- Allergies: yes / no + detail
- Broviac / Port-a-cath: yes / no + detail  <-- CREW-SPECIFIC, campers do not have this
- Eyesight
- Hearing
- Mobility aids
- Prosthesis
- Other

## TB screening triplet
- A cough that lasts longer than 2 weeks
- Unexplained weight loss
- Night sweats or unexplained fevers

## Assistance with daily living (checkbox + per-item)
- Shower / bath
- Dressing
- Toileting
- Eating

## Medication
- Medication: yes / no
- Blood count: yes / no  <-- CREW-SPECIFIC
- Date
- Other comments (free text)

## Sign-off
- Camp crew name (print) + signature + date
- Medical person name (print) + signature + date

The medical release policy text (referral, emergency authorisation, fitness attestation)
is acknowledgement content shown above the sign-off, not stored fields.
